Generations of Devotion

The Vistara Legacy

Founded in 1988 with a single wooden pit loom, Vistara has grown into a sanctuary for India's finest handloom master weavers.

Our Philosophy

We Do Not Make Fast Fashion. We Weave Family Heirlooms.

In an era dominated by synthetic powerlooms and polyester blends, Vistara remains unyielding in its devotion to the slow art of the shuttle and warp. A genuine handloom saree does not merely dress a woman—it envelops her in the sacred lineage of her ancestors.

Each Vistara saree takes between 18 days to 4 months to craft. Two master artisans work in synchrony—one guiding the wooden warp pedals, while the other manually slides the silver-gold zari shuttles through intricate jacquard punches. When you touch a Vistara silk, you feel the soul and dignity of its maker.

"A saree is six yards of pure poetry, woven with devotion, worn with royalty."

— The Founders of Vistara

Preserving Your Heirloom

The Saree Connoisseur Care Guide

Pure mulberry silks and authentic gold zari require gentle reverence to last for generations.

01. Muslin Storage

Wrap your silk sarees in breathable, unbleached pure cotton or muslin fabric. Never store silk in plastic covers which trap humidity.

02. Periodic Refolding

Every 3 to 4 months, take your sarees out, air them in a shaded breeze, and refold them along opposite creases to prevent permanent fold tears.

03. Dry Clean Only

Always entrust pure silk and zari sarees to specialized professional dry cleaners. Never expose zari to water or direct harsh detergents.

04. No Direct Perfume

Apply your fragrance and allow it to dry completely before draping. Direct alcohol droplets can react with real silver and gold zari threads.
