Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our silk certification, customization, and worldwide delivery.

How do I know my Vistara saree is genuine handloom silk? +
Every Vistara drape is tagged with an authentic Silk Mark India label, containing a unique QR verification code backed by the Central Silk Board. Moreover, authentic handloom sarees possess subtle variations in weave tension and selvedge edges that testify to the touch of human hands.
What type of zari is used in Vistara sarees? +
Our heirloom bridal collection utilizes pure silver electroplated with 24K gold zari. Our festive collection uses high-grade tested zari that retains its brilliant sheen without tarnishing or turning black over time when properly stored in muslin.
Does the saree come with a blouse piece? +
Yes. Every saree includes an attached 0.8-meter matching or contrast unstitched blouse piece. We also offer bespoke custom stitching by our bridal master tailors with personalized necklines, sleeve embroidery, and measurements.
How does international shipping work? +
We deliver worldwide across 45+ countries (USA, UK, Canada, UAE, Singapore, Australia, etc.) via insured DHL Express. Orders above ₹50,000 enjoy complimentary express shipping. Typical delivery takes 4 to 6 business days.
