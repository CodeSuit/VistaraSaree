The Art of Weaving

The Soul of the Loom

No industrial machines. No synthetic polyester. Only the rhythmic cadence of master weavers preserving centuries-old royal lineages.

Certified Authenticity Every Vistara drape arrives with an individual Silk Mark hologram and certificate of authenticity.
01

Mulberry Silk Selection

We source only unbroken, high-twist grade AAA mulberry silk filaments, ensuring an unmatched lustre and decades-long resilience.

02

Real Silver & Gold Zari Spinning

Our zari uses pure silver wire flattened and electroplated in 24K pure gold, creating that unmistakable antique glow that never tarnishes.

03

Korvai & Kadwa Handloom Technique

Two weavers sit side by side on traditional wooden pit looms, interlocking contrasting borders without a single break in thread continuity.

04

Fair Trade Artisan Guilds

By eliminating middle trading agents, Vistara guarantees direct premium payouts and healthcare pensions to over 1,200 weaver families.

Styling Masterclass

The Four Regal Drapes

How to style your Vistara saree for ceremonies, cocktail soirees, and traditional weddings.

Bridal Classic

The Royal Nivi Drape

The timeless drape featuring neat waist pleats and an open, cascading pallu pinned lightly over the shoulder.

Styling Tip: Ideal for heavy Kanjivaram and Banarasi sarees paired with antique temple jewellery.
Editorial Glamour

The Belted Modern Drape

A structured, runway-inspired drape cinched at the waist with an ornate embroidered kamarbandh or metallic belt.

Styling Tip: Perfect for Organza and Chanderi sarees at reception galas and sangeet evenings.
Aristocratic Heritage

The Bengali Athpourey

Box pleats in front with the rich pallu draped gracefully from back to front over both shoulders with an ornate bunch of keys.

Styling Tip: Highlights heavy zari pallus and Tanchoi Banarasi weaves with regal grandeur.
Festive Elegance

The Seedha Pallu (Gujarati)

The ornate pallu is brought from the back over the right shoulder to show off intricate tapestry motifs across the front chest.

Styling Tip: The best choice for Paithani and Shikargah Banarasi masterpieces with pictorial birds.
